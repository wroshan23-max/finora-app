Finora User Guide

How to get the most out of Finora — Manage Money. Grow Wealth.

Finora is built around the 6 Jars money management system from The Secrets of the Millionaire Mind: every time you log income, it's automatically split across six purpose-driven "jars" so your money has a job before you spend it. This guide walks through every part of the app.

🚀Quick Start

  1. Go to the Accounts tab and add your first account — Cash, a bank account, or a credit card — with its current balance.
  2. Go to Dashboard and use the Add a Transaction card to log your first income or expense.
  3. When you log income, Finora automatically splits it across the 6 Jars using the percentages in the 6 Jars & Freedom tab — adjust those percentages to match your own plan.
  4. Check the Dashboard for your Financial Health Score, Net Worth, and jar balances at a glance.
  5. Turn on Cloud Sync in Settings so your data backs up automatically and follows you across devices.

🏦Accounts

Accounts are where your money actually lives — Cash, a savings account, a current account, a credit card, or a foreign-currency account. Add one from the Accounts tab with a name, type, and its current balance (your opening balance "as of" a chosen date).

  • Credit cards are tracked as a balance owed — payments to them are logged as a transfer into the card account, not as an expense.
  • You can hide an account (e.g. an old, unused one) so it's excluded from your Main Balance while still counting toward Net Worth.
  • Accounts in a foreign currency show up separately on the Dashboard under Foreign Currency Assets, converted to your Home Currency using the exchange rate you set on that account.

➕Adding Transactions

Use the Add a Transaction card (Dashboard or Transactions tab) for every bit of money coming in or going out.

  • Income — pick the account it landed in, amount, source, and date. It's automatically split into the 6 Jars, shown as a live preview before you save.
  • Expense — pick the account it came from, amount, category, and which jar it should come out of.
  • Business Income / Business Expense — tick "This is Business Income" (or the expense equivalent) to keep it in a separate Business pool instead of the 6 Jars — useful if you run a side business alongside personal finances.
  • Don't split into the 6 Jars — for a one-off income amount you don't want budgeted (e.g. a gift you're setting aside separately), tick this and it still counts toward Total Income but skips the jar split.
  • The amount field accepts quick sums like 100+50-20 — handy when you're adding up a few receipts.

🔁Transfers Between Accounts

Moving money between your own accounts (e.g. Cash → Bank, or paying off a credit card) is a Transfer, not an income or expense — it doesn't touch your jar budgets, since no new money entered or left your total wealth.

  • If your bank charges a fee for the transfer, add it in the Bank charge / fee field — that fee is real money spent, so it's automatically counted under Total Expense as a "Bank Charges" category (while the transfer amount itself still isn't).
  • Paying off a credit card balance is simply a transfer into that card account.

📉Liabilities & Bill Reminders

Track loans, pawning, or other debts (separate from credit cards, which live under Accounts) in the Liabilities tab — name, current balance owed, interest rate, and minimum payment.

  • Set a Due day of month on a liability to get an automatic reminder banner on the Dashboard as the payment date approaches (🟡 due soon, 🟠 due within 2 days, 🔴 overdue) — this only shows when you actually open the app, since it's a client-side app with no server to push a real notification.
  • The Debt Settlement Planner compares Avalanche (highest interest first) vs. Snowball (smallest balance first) payoff strategies and estimates how many months until you're debt-free at your chosen extra monthly payment.
Not sure which strategy to pick? Both work the same way — pay the minimum on every debt, then throw every spare rupee at one target debt first, then roll that whole payment onto the next once it's cleared. They only differ in which debt goes first: Avalanche targets the highest interest rate first, which is mathematically the cheapest path out of debt overall. Snowball targets the smallest balance first — it can cost a little more in interest, but clearing a whole debt quickly builds momentum, which is often what actually keeps people going. Finora shows both side by side (with the one that saves more money marked) so you can pick whichever fits how you stay motivated. Read more: Fidelity — debt snowball vs. debt avalanche.

🏺The 6 Jars & Financial Freedom

The 6 Jars — Necessities, Financial Freedom Account, Long-Term Savings, Education, Play, and Give — each get a percentage of every income transaction. Adjust the percentages in the 6 Jars & Freedom tab to match your own plan (they should add up to 100%).

New to the 6 Jars idea? It's a simple money-management method popularised by T. Harv Eker in his book Secrets of the Millionaire Mind: instead of one pool of money, every rupee of income is split, right away, into six labelled "jars" — Necessities (daily living costs), Financial Freedom Account (long-term investing, never spent), Long-Term Savings (bigger planned purchases), Education (learning/self-improvement), Play (guilt-free fun), and Give (charity/gifts). The point isn't the exact percentages — it's that every goal gets its own share automatically, so saving and giving happen by default instead of "whatever's left over." Finora automates this split for you on every income entry. Read more: T. Harv Eker — the 6 Jars money management system.
  • Set a Financial Freedom target — the amount you're aiming to build in your Financial Freedom Account — and track progress toward it.
  • The Pay Yourself First prompt appears after logging income, reminding you to move your Financial Freedom share out before spending anything else (can be turned off in Settings if it feels unnecessary).

📊Budget Limits

Set an optional monthly spending limit per jar or per expense category in 6 Jars & Freedom → Budget Limits. A progress bar turns amber near the limit and red once you've gone over it — purely a visual nudge, nothing is blocked.

🔁Recurring Transactions

For things that repeat every month — rent, salary, subscriptions — set up a template once in the Recurring Transactions card (Transactions tab): name, amount, account, and frequency. Finora automatically logs the real transaction each time you open the app after it comes due, with a confirmation toast. Pause or delete a template anytime; past transactions it already created are untouched.

🎯Savings Goals

Break your Long-Term Savings jar into named sub-goals (e.g. "Emergency Fund", "New Laptop") with a target amount, so you can see progress toward each one individually rather than just one lump balance.

📈Reports & Insights

  • The Reports tab gives Income/Expense/Net/Savings Rate for any month, year, or (Pro) custom date range, plus a category breakdown and trend-over-time chart.
  • PRO Advanced Reports add a by-account breakdown, category spend trend over the last 6 periods, and side-by-side period comparison.
  • 📄 Full Report (button at the top of the Reports tab) puts everything into one polished, printable document for the period you've selected: Financial Health Score, Net Worth Statement, Cash Flow, Spending Analysis, Budget Performance, Debt & Liabilities, Goals & Fixed Obligations, Behavioral Insights, Top Transactions, and a full Transaction List — ready to save as a PDF.
  • Tap ⚙️ Customize inside the Full Report to show or hide any of its sections — handy if you only want, say, Net Worth and Spending Analysis without the rest. This only decides which sections appear; the report's look and layout stay exactly the same either way, and your choice is remembered for next time.
  • The "View Report" buttons on the Assets, Liabilities, and Lending tabs open the Full Report and jump straight to the relevant section (Net Worth or Debt & Liabilities) — even if you'd previously hidden it with Customize, it's shown again automatically so you always land somewhere useful.
  • Spending Alerts on the Dashboard automatically flag a category if its spending this month-so-far is up 25%+ versus the same number of days last month (e.g. "Food & Groceries is up 42% vs. this point last month") — a quick nudge, not a full report.
  • Tap any stat tile (Total Income, Total Expense, etc.) to drill into exactly which transactions make it up.

🏆Financial Health Score

A 0–100 score on the Dashboard combining your Savings Rate, Net Worth health (assets vs. debt), and Debt Payoff Progress into one general check-in indicator. It's a simplified heuristic, not financial advice.

☁️Backup: Save/Load, Cloud Sync & Google Drive

  • Save Data / Load Data (top of every page) downloads or restores a local backup file — works with no account at all.
  • Cloud Sync (Settings) creates a free account so your data backs up automatically and follows you to any device you sign in on.
  • Google Drive Backup (Settings, Pro) saves a backup copy directly to your own Google Drive.

👨‍👩‍👧Shared Household PRO

Invite family members by email to full view + edit access on your synced data — they don't need their own Pro subscription while working inside your household. Manage or revoke invites anytime from Settings.

⭐Finora Pro

Pro removes ads, unlocks editing past entries, Advanced Reports, Google Drive Backup, and Shared Household. Subscribe from Settings — 🇱🇰 PayHere for LKR, or 🌍 card/Apple Pay/Google Pay via Dodo Payments for everyone else. Cancel anytime; you keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.

🎁Refer a Friend

Every signed-in account has its own referral link, shown under Settings → Refer a Friend — copy it or tap 📤 Share to send it straight to WhatsApp or wherever you'd share a link. The link doesn't drop your friend cold into the app — it opens a short intro page about Finora and the reward first, with a clear "Get Started Free" button that takes them straight to sign-up. Anyone who creates a new Finora account through your link earns you both a reward:

  • Once your friend has signed up and actually started using the app (added an account and logged at least one transaction, a few days after signing up), you both get 5 bonus days of Finora Pro, free — no card, no strings attached.
  • This check happens automatically in the background the next time either of you has the app open — there's nothing to claim manually.
  • Bonus Pro days are capped at 60 per person per year, so there's always a reason to eventually subscribe for real if Finora's earning a permanent place in your routine.
  • Your Settings page shows a running count of how many friends have signed up through your link and how many have earned you a reward.
The referral reward currently only triggers when your friend signs up using Email + Password (under Cloud Sync → "prefer something else?"). Signing up with Google doesn't attribute a referral yet — if that matters to you, ask your friend to use the email option instead.

📱Installing Finora as an App

Finora can be installed like a native app, with an icon on your home screen and the ability to open with no internet connection:

  • Android (Chrome): open finora.com.lk, tap the menu (⋮), and choose Add to Home Screen or Install app.
  • iPhone/iPad (Safari): open finora.com.lk, tap the Share icon, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Desktop (Chrome/Edge): look for the install icon in the address bar.
Cloud Sync, Google Drive, and ads all need an internet connection as usual — but the app itself opens instantly offline once installed.

🌍Multiple Currencies

Set your Home Currency from the dropdown at the top of the app — every total on the Dashboard and Reports is shown in this currency. Individual accounts can be held in a different currency (with their own exchange rate to Home Currency), useful for a foreign bank account or a USD wallet.

🔒Privacy & Security

  • With no Cloud Sync account, everything stays only in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
  • An optional App Password (Settings) guards the "Clear All Data" action against an accidental tap — it's a local safety net, not a full app lock, and is never sent anywhere or synced to the cloud.
  • Full details in the Privacy Policy.

❓FAQ

Why doesn't a transfer count as an expense?

Because it isn't one — moving Rs 10,000 from Cash to your bank account doesn't make you poorer, it's the same money in a different place. Only a transfer's bank fee, if any, is real money spent, so that part is counted.

Can I edit or delete a past transaction?

Yes — editing past entries is a Pro feature (adding new ones is always free). Every delete asks for confirmation first, unless you've turned that off in Settings (Pro).

What happens to my data if I stop paying for Pro?

Nothing is deleted. You simply lose access to the Pro-only features (Advanced Reports, editing past entries, Drive Backup, Household invites, and ads come back) — all your data stays exactly as it was.
