This Privacy Policy explains what information Finora ("the App", "we") collects when you use it, why, and how it's handled. Finora is operated by Taxmate Solutions (wroshan23@gmail.com).

What we collect

Finora is designed to keep as little of your data on our servers as possible:

  • If you never sign in: your financial data (transactions, accounts, liabilities) stays only in your own browser and is never sent to us. Nothing about your money is collected.
  • If you create an account (Cloud Sync): your email address, and the financial data you choose to sync, are stored so you can access it across your devices. This is handled by our database provider, Supabase.
  • If you subscribe to Finora Pro: your subscription status (active/cancelled) is stored so the app knows to unlock Pro features. Payment details themselves (card numbers etc.) are handled entirely by our payment processor, PayHere — we never see or store your card details.
  • If you use Shared Household (Finora Pro): you can invite other people, by email, to view and edit your synced financial data — for example a partner or family member. We store the email address you invite so the app can recognize them when they sign in. Anyone you invite gets full access to your synced data until you revoke it, so only invite people you trust with that information. You can revoke access at any time from Settings.
  • If you use Google Drive Backup: a backup file is written to your own Google Drive, using a narrow permission that only lets Finora see files it created itself — not the rest of your Drive.

Third parties involved

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in, if you use Cloud Sync.
  • PayHere — processes subscription payments. See PayHere's privacy policy.
  • Google (AdSense) — if you're on the free plan, Finora may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technology to personalize ads based on your activity. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Pro subscribers do not see ads.
  • Google Drive — only if you explicitly connect it for backups, and only for the backup file Finora itself creates.

Your choices

You can use Finora entirely offline without ever creating an account. If you've signed in, you can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting wroshan23@gmail.com. Cancelling a Pro subscription is available directly in Settings.

Data retention

Your synced data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your stored data.
